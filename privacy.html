Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

This policy describes how TORQWISE (“we,” “us,” or “the service”) handles information when you use the automotive and equipment research website. Before public launch, the operator must confirm the legal business identity and contact details that apply to this service.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we process your email address, account identifier, and any name you choose to provide. If you submit a research request, we process the vehicle or equipment details, VIN or serial information you choose to submit, symptoms, questions, requested deadlines, and request status. The service records research usage and subscription status.

Do not submit passwords for other services, payment-card numbers, government identifiers, or personal information that is not needed for vehicle or equipment research.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate your account, provide and save research, enforce free-plan limits, manage subscriptions, respond to submitted requests, prevent abuse, maintain security, and improve service reliability.

Service providers

TORQWISE is designed to use Supabase for authentication and database storage, Stripe for subscription checkout and billing management, and OpenAI for AI-assisted research. Information sent to these providers is handled under their applicable terms and privacy policies. Payment card details are entered into Stripe-hosted checkout; TORQWISE should not collect or store full card numbers.

VINs, serial numbers, and research content

Vehicle identification numbers, equipment serial numbers, symptoms, and repair questions may be sent to the research service to prepare an answer. Submit only information you are authorized to share. Research answers may include links to third-party sources and may not confirm exact applicability to a specific vehicle or machine.

Retention and security

Account records, submitted requests, and research usage may be retained while needed to operate the service, maintain account history, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and prevent fraud. We use access controls and server-side credentials, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Do not treat the service as an OEM service-record archive.

Your choices

You may stop using the service and manage an active subscription through the subscription-management portal. Account deletion and data-access requests require a verified process from the service operator. Before public launch, the operator must publish a monitored support contact and implement a reliable way to handle privacy requests.

Children and changes

The service is intended for adults and professional users, not children. This policy may be updated as the service changes; the effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.

Launch configuration required: The operator must add a verified support/privacy contact and confirm the legal business identity and applicable jurisdiction before accepting public customers. This page is a working policy draft, not legal advice.
