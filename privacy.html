Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

This policy describes how TORQWISE (“we,” “us,” or “the service”) handles information when you use the TORQWISE website and planned native mobile app. The app may store your garage list, saved diagnostic cases, and a cached generic fault-code reference library locally on your device. Before public launch, the operator must confirm the legal business identity and contact details that apply to this service.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we process your email address and account identifier. If you submit AI research, we process the vehicle or equipment details, VIN or serial information you choose to submit, symptoms, questions, test results, and request content. The service records research usage and subscription status. The native app stores garage entries and saved diagnostic cases locally; these are not currently synced to your account. The app may cache generic diagnostic-code reference data on the device.

Do not submit passwords for other services, payment-card numbers, government identifiers, or personal information that is not needed for vehicle or equipment research.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate your account, provide AI-assisted research, enforce usage limits, manage subscriptions, respond to submitted requests, prevent abuse, maintain security, and improve service reliability. Local garage entries and saved diagnostic cases provide device-only organization features.

Service providers

TORQWISE uses Supabase for account authentication, OpenAI for server-side AI-assisted research, and Stripe for website subscription checkout and billing where enabled. VIN decoding sends the submitted VIN to the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) vPIC service. Research requests are sent to TORQWISE servers and may be sent to OpenAI and enabled research tools. Payment card details entered into Stripe-hosted checkout are handled by Stripe. Native-app purchases must be described accurately here before they are enabled.

VINs, serial numbers, and research content

Vehicle identification numbers, equipment serial numbers, symptoms, and repair questions may be sent to the research service to prepare an answer. When using VIN decoding, the VIN is sent to NHTSA vPIC. Submit only information you are authorized to share. Research answers may include third-party links and may not confirm exact applicability to a specific vehicle or machine.

Retention and security

Account records, submitted requests, and research usage may be retained while needed to operate the service, maintain account history, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and prevent fraud. We use access controls and server-side credentials, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Do not treat the service as an OEM service-record archive.

Your choices

You can remove garage entries and saved diagnostic cases from the app’s Garage screen; this clears those local lists from the device but does not delete your online account. You may manage an active website subscription through the available subscription-management portal. Account deletion and requests to access or delete server-side data require a verified process from the operator. Before public launch, the operator must publish a monitored support contact and implement a reliable account-deletion and privacy-request process.

Children and changes

The service is intended for adults and professional users, not children. This policy may be updated as the service changes; the effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.

Launch configuration required: Review this policy against the final app and actual data flows. The operator must add a verified support/privacy contact, confirm legal business identity and jurisdiction, implement account deletion, and verify any in-app purchase disclosures before public launch. This is a working policy draft, not legal advice.
