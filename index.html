Vehicle identification

Decode a VIN.

Look up the VIN against the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration's public vPIC decoder. An internet connection is required; incomplete or non-U.S.-market VINs may return limited data.

VINs normally use 17 characters and exclude I, O and Q. Do not use this result alone to confirm parts fitment.

Data source: NHTSA vPIC public API. This lookup is not an OEM parts catalogue and may not identify every option or engine variant.
