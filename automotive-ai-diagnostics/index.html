Automotive AI diagnostics and repair research

TORQWISE brings diagnostic research, OBD-II trouble-code information, VIN lookup, parts fitment research, and repair planning into one workspace. It is designed to help vehicle owners, DIY mechanics, apprentices, and technicians organize the next steps when investigating a vehicle problem.

What can you research?

OBD-II trouble codes

Look up diagnostic trouble codes, review possible system causes, and structure further checks. A code is a starting point for diagnosis, not proof that a particular part has failed.

VIN lookup and vehicle research

Use vehicle identification details to help establish vehicle information before parts research or repair work.

Parts fitment research

Organize parts research around vehicle details and fitment considerations. Confirm compatibility with supplier catalogues and manufacturer information where required.

Repair planning

Bring symptoms, diagnostic findings, and research together to plan sensible next checks and communicate findings more clearly.

Built for informed troubleshooting

Good diagnostics combines symptoms, fault codes, service information, inspection, and measurements. TORQWISE helps organize research; it does not replace qualified technicians, vehicle-specific service information, or safety procedures.
